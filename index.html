What the 60 minutes covers

The five reasons for bad quality leads

It's a live session with Michael with Q&A at the end – not a recording. The goal is for you to leave with things you can fix today.

1

Wrong customer

How ads end up reaching people who can't buy, and what to look at in your targeting, your search terms and your last 20 leads.

2

Wrong message

How the promise in your ad copy attracts the wrong person, shown with one anonymised before and after.

3

Wrong channel

How to tell whether the channel fits the way your customer buys.

4

Weak creative

Whether your ad looks credible enough to stop the right buyer. You'll see a weak ad next to a strong one.

5

Weak landing page

Whether the page behind your ad backs it up, or loses the right buyer once they click. You'll see a weak page next to a strong one.

This webinar is for you if:

  • You're paying for leads you wouldn't take a call with. And you take the call anyway, in case this one's real.

  • Your agency says leads are up. Your sales team says they're junk. You're stuck in the middle, paying for both.

  • You've thought about firing the agency. You just can't say for sure the fault is theirs, and starting again with someone new feels worse.

  • Your ad spend keeps going up, but your revenue doesn't. Every month you put more in to get the same handful of real buyers out, and you're running out of ways to justify it.
